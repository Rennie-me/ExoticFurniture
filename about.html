OUR STORY

Furniture should feel like coming home.

Based in Westlands, Nairobi, Exotic Furniture Business Centre is a destination for people who want to make their homes more comfortable, welcoming and personal. We bring together thoughtfully selected furniture for living, dining and resting, with a focus on quality, style and service.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Less rush. More meaning.

A home comes together over time. That’s why we look for the balance of beautiful shapes, useful details and inviting comfort—not just another passing trend.

From a first apartment to a much-loved family home, we want finding the right furniture to feel personal, considered and refreshingly simple.
